About HA Unity Industries

HA Unity Industries is a clothing and sportswear manufacturing company serving businesses, brands, and organisations. We focus on quality production, reliable communication, and flexible order volumes.

From custom hoodies and T-shirts to embroidered skirts and performance sportswear, we work with customer-provided designs and logos. Whether you need a small batch or bulk wholesale production, we are set up to support your requirements.

  • Custom manufacturing
  • Bulk & wholesale orders
  • Customer designs accepted
  • Printing & embroidery
  • B2B focused service
  • Responsive communication

Manufacturing & Customisation

We handle the production process from your specifications to finished garments. Here is how we work with you.

01

Design & Tech Pack Review

Share your designs, logos, and specifications. We review and confirm feasibility, materials, and timelines before production.

02

Fabric & Material Selection

Choose from a range of fabrics, weights, and finishes. We help match materials to your intended use and price point.

03

Custom Printing & Embroidery

Screen printing, DTG, sublimation, and embroidery. We apply your artwork with precision and consistency across the order.

04

Sampling & Approval

We produce samples for your approval before full production, ensuring the fit, feel, and finish meet your expectations.

05

Bulk Production

Once approved, we proceed with bulk manufacturing with quality checks throughout the process.

06

Packing & Dispatch

Finished garments are packed according to your requirements and prepared for dispatch or pickup.

Wholesale & Bulk Orders

1 Minimum Order Quantities

MOQs vary by product and customisation. Contact us with your requirements for a specific quote.
